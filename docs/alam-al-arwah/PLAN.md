# ʿĀlam al-Arwāḥ: Animation Plan

> **Superseded (11 Nov 2026).** The real plan, in the Iqra house style, is in `adamisharuddin/iqra.signs`: `episodes/sign-008/TREATMENT.md`.

A 9:16 short-form animation (TikTok / Reels / Shorts), about 70 s, based on a 7-slide reference carousel about the Realm of Souls, the Primordial Covenant (al-Mīthāq), fitrah, and why humans forget.

---

## 1. What the reference does well (keep)

| Element | Why it works | How we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| **One still figure in a rushing crowd** | The core metaphor: the soul that remembers, standing among people who rush past and have forgotten. | Make it the main visual. The figure never moves and the crowd never stops, until the Mīthāq beat. |
| **Golden-hour old-city alley** | Warm, sacred, timeless. The light coming down from above the alley reads as "from above". | Keep the palette. Use the light source as a story device that grows during the covenant. |
| **White rounded text labels** | A native TikTok look that is easy to read on mobile. | Keep the style, but animate the labels line by line in sync with the voiceover. |
| **Strong hook question** | Stops the scroll. | Keep the shape, but fix the theology (see §2). |

## 2. Content corrections (must fix before production)

The reference makes several claims that are overstated or theologically risky. A channel called *iqra* should get these right.

| # | Reference says | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How old were you when you realized this **isn't your first life**?" | Reads as reincarnation (*tanāsukh*), which Islamic theology rejects. ʿĀlam al-Arwāḥ is an earlier **stage** of existence, not a past life. | "How old were you when you realised **this isn't where your story began**?" |
| 2 | "Insān derives **directly** from nasiya (to forget)" | This is one classical opinion, attributed to Ibn ʿAbbās. Many linguists derive *insān* from *uns* (intimacy, sociability). | "Some early scholars, like Ibn ʿAbbās, linked *insān* to *nasiya*, to forget, recalling Adam: *'…but he forgot'* (Ṭā Hā 20:115)." |
| 3 | Hadith: "…those who recognized each other **there** will feel drawn to each other **here**." | "There/here" is added interpretation, and the second half of the hadith is left out. | "Souls are like assembled troops. Those that knew one another are drawn together, and those that did not, drift apart." *(Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2638; al-Bukhārī)* |
| 4 | "Your intellect, personality, and spiritual signature were **already fully alive**." | Speculative and stated as fact. | "Every soul that would ever live was brought forth and made to bear witness." *(al-Aʿrāf 7:172)* |
| 5 | "Prophet Muhammad ﷺ **unlocked this exact secret**" | Clickbait framing of a hadith. | "The Prophet ﷺ said:" |
| 6 | "Faith would require **zero effort**" | Fine in spirit, but 7:172–173 says the covenant was taken so that no one can say "we were unaware". A trace of it remains: the fitrah. | Keep the idea and add: "You forgot the moment, but not the imprint." |
| 7 | Slide 4 ends mid-sentence ("It is your Fitrah") | Unfinished. | "…It is your fitrah, remembering what it already knew." *(ar-Rūm 30:30)* |

**Visual adab:** never depict the Prophet ﷺ, angels, or the Divine. Show the Divine address only through light and Qur'anic text.

**Rights:** the reference image and text belong to @path2jannaah. Do **not** reuse their image, watermark, or exact wording. Use our own background (generated, licensed stock, or our own footage) and the rewritten script below.

## 3. Slide order → story order

The 7 images arrived out of order. This is the story order:

1. Hook: "How old were you…" (img 3)
2. The map of 5 realms (img 6)
3. The gathering and al-Mīthāq (img 5)
4. "This single event changes everything", fitrah (img 4)
5. "So why don't you remember?", forgetting (img 1)
6. Strangers who feel familiar, the hadith (img 2)
7. Closer: "You've been on this journey a long time" (img 7)

Moving the hadith (scene 6) after "why you forget" (scene 5) makes the ending stronger. Scene 5 asks "if I forgot, why do I still feel it?", and scene 6 answers with lived experience before the closer.

## 4. Script and shot list (target ~70 s, ~150 wpm VO)

Format: **1080×1920, 30 fps**. Keep text inside the TikTok safe zone (top 150 px, bottom 420 px, and right 140 px are covered by UI).

### S1 · Hook (0:00–0:05)
- **VO:** "How old were you when you realised… this isn't where your story began?"
- **On screen:** Uppercase label pops in, then a second label: *ʿĀlam al-Arwāḥ, the Realm of Souls.*
- **Motion:** Slow push-in on the alley. The crowd streaks past. The figure is perfectly still. Dust motes drift in the light.
- **Sound:** Low drone and a soft whoosh as each label appears.

### S2 · The Map (0:05–0:17)
- **VO:** "Most people think life begins at birth and ends at death. But in Islam, your existence moves through five realms. And right now, you're in the third."
- **On screen:** A vertical timeline of 5 nodes, drawn in a thin gold line. Each node lights up as it's named:
  1. ʿĀlam al-Arwāḥ (Realm of Souls)
  2. al-Raḥm (the Womb)
  3. al-Dunyā (this world) ← **"You are here"** pulses
  4. al-Barzakh (the in-between) · *23:100*
  5. al-Ākhirah (the Hereafter)
- **Motion:** The background dims and blurs to 40%. The timeline draws in top to bottom. The camera "drops" from node 1 to node 3.

### S3 · The Covenant (0:17–0:32): the centrepiece
- **VO:** "Before any of us was born, every soul that would ever live was brought forth from the children of Adam. And Allah asked them: *Alastu bi-Rabbikum?* Am I not your Lord? And they said: *Balā, shahidnā.* Yes, we bear witness."
- **On screen:**
  - Arabic calligraphy fades in: **أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ** → beat → **قَالُوا بَلَىٰ شَهِدْنَا**
  - Transliteration and translation in labels beneath it. Small citation: *al-Aʿrāf 7:172*
- **Motion (the key moment):** The crowd **freezes** mid-stride. Their motion blur breaks into thousands of light particles that rise toward the light source, which blooms to near white. On "Balā", every particle pulses together once (the "unison" answer). Then everything settles back.
- **Sound:** The drone stops for silence before "Alastu". A single resonant tone on "Balā".

### S4 · Fitrah (0:32–0:42)
- **VO:** "This changes everything. Faith, truth, and love aren't things you invent from scratch. They're things you recognise. Real ma'rifah isn't learning something new. It's your fitrah, remembering what it already knew."
- **On screen:** Labels stack one by one. The word **"recognise"** is highlighted gold. Citation *ar-Rūm 30:30*.
- **Motion:** The particles drift down and settle **into the still figure**, giving it a faint inner glow. The crowd resumes moving.

### S5 · Why we forget (0:42–0:52)
- **VO:** "So why don't you remember? Some early scholars linked the word *insān*, human, to *nasiya*, to forget, just as the Qur'an says of Adam: *'he forgot.'* If you remembered that moment clearly, the test of this life would disappear. Belief would cost nothing. Choice would weigh nothing. You forgot the moment, but not the imprint."
- **On screen:** The word **إنسان** with the letters **ن س ي** highlighted, then a small **فَنَسِيَ** with *Ṭā Hā 20:115*.
- **Motion:** The crowd speeds up and blurs more ("the world pulls"). The figure's glow dims but **does not go out** (a small ember stays at the chest).

### S6 · Strangers (0:52–1:02)
- **VO:** "Ever met a stranger and felt, within minutes, like you've always known them? The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Souls are like assembled troops. Those that knew one another are drawn together, and those that did not, drift apart.'"
- **On screen:** Hadith in Arabic (small) and English (label). Citation *Muslim 2638*.
- **Motion:** A **second** still figure appears across the alley. The crowd blurs between them. A thin thread of light connects their two embers. No faces are shown; both are seen from behind or in silhouette.

### S7 · Closer (1:02–1:10)
- **VO:** "You didn't begin twenty or thirty years ago. You're a soul on a long journey, passing through a test. Every pull toward truth is just a reminder… of a promise you already made."
- **On screen:** The final label holds, then the **iqra** end card and a soft CTA ("Save this, and send it to someone you felt you'd always known.").
- **Motion:** A slow pull-back up the alley toward the light. The crowd fades out, leaving only the still figure(s). Fade to warm white, then the logo.

## 5. Visual system

- **Palette:** deep umber `#1A120B`, amber `#C8892E`, gold light `#F5C77A`, near-white bloom `#FFF4E0`. Labels are white `#FFFFFF` with black text, 24 px radius, and a slight drop shadow.
- **Type:** Latin is a clean grotesk (Inter / Manrope Semibold) to match the reference labels. Arabic is Amiri or Scheherazade New with full diacritics.
- **Label animation:** each line scales from 0.96 to 1, fades in, and moves up 8 px (~250 ms ease-out), timed to VO word timestamps. The previous labels dim to 60% when a new block starts.
- **Background build (2.5D parallax)**, as separate layers:
  1. Sky and light source (animated bloom and god-rays)
  2. Alley walls and street (static plate, slow camera move)
  3. Crowd: a looping blurred-pedestrian video layer, or a particle crowd for the freeze-and-dissolve moment
  4. Still figure (cut-out; gets the glow and ember effects)
  5. Foreground blurred passers-by (fast streaks across the frame)
  6. Dust and light particles, film grain, vignette

## 6. Production pipeline

| Step | Tool | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lock script | This doc | Final VO text, checked by someone qualified in ʿaqīdah/ḥadīth |
| 2. Voiceover | **ElevenLabs** (connected) with a calm, warm, measured voice, ~140–150 wpm. Generate **with word timestamps**. | `vo.mp3` and `vo.timestamps.json` |
| 3. Arabic recitation (optional) | A recorded short recitation of 7:172 for S3, or have the VO read the Arabic phrases | `alastu.wav` |
| 4. Background plates | Our own generated, licensed, or filmed golden-hour alley; separate the layers | `/assets/bg/*` |
| 5. Animation | **Remotion** (React → MP4). This keeps the video as code in this repo. Labels are driven by the VO timestamps, so retiming is automatic. | `src/` compositions per scene |
| 6. SFX and music | Ambient drone, whooshes, a single tone (no-instrument or nasheed-safe options) | `/assets/audio/*` |
| 7. Render | `npx remotion render` at 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 | `out/alam-al-arwah.mp4` |
| 8. Burned-in captions | Already on screen through the labels; also export `.srt` | `out/*.srt` |
